Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This policy explains what data Nexo Publisher, the internal publishing tool of Nexo Commerce, accesses when it is connected to a TikTok account. The tool is used only with TikTok accounts owned by Nexo Commerce.

2. Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the authorized account (open ID, display name, avatar), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos on that account, and the status of each upload.

We do not access followers' data, private messages, comments of other users, contacts or location.

3. How we use and store it

The access token issued by TikTok is stored on a single private computer controlled by Nexo Commerce and is used only to publish our own videos. We do not sell, share or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while publishing is active and are deleted when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app, in Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions. Revoking access makes the token unusable immediately.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask about, correct or delete any personal data we hold. Nexo Publisher holds data only about Nexo Commerce's own accounts, but you can contact us with any request.

6. Contact

Send a direct message to @nexo_comerciales on Instagram or TikTok.
